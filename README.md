# HerPath 🌱

### Her Skills. Her Journey. Her Future.

**HerPath** is an AI-powered skill ecosystem designed to help women move from **learning skills to creating opportunities and building financial independence**.

It connects personalized learning, skill discovery, mentorship, portfolios, skill exchange, and opportunities into one ecosystem.

> **Learn → Practice → Showcase → Connect → Opportunity → Earn → Teach**

---

## 🚀 What is HerPath?

Many women have skills, interests, and aspirations but face difficulties finding the **right learning resources, mentors, communities, and opportunities** to turn those skills into meaningful outcomes.

HerPath addresses this gap by creating a personalized digital pathway for every user.

Instead of simply recommending courses or jobs, HerPath understands:

* What the user already knows
* What they want to learn
* What their goals are
* What they can teach
* Their preferred language
* Their location
* Their availability
* Their interests

It then connects these relationships to recommend the **right people, skills, content, mentors, and opportunities**.

---

# 🎯 The Problem

Women often face multiple barriers while trying to build digital and professional skills:

* Difficulty finding relevant learning resources
* Limited access to mentors and experts
* Lack of visibility for their existing skills
* Difficulty demonstrating skills through a professional portfolio
* Limited access to relevant opportunities
* Language and digital-access barriers
* Disconnect between learning and real-world opportunities
* Difficulty finding people with complementary skills

Most existing platforms solve only one part of this journey.

# 5. AI-Powered Matching

HerPath does not simply show random recommendations.

Each recommendation can explain:

```text

```

This makes recommendations transparent and understandable.

# 6. Knowledge Graph

HerPath models relationships between users, skills, projects, content, mentors, and opportunities.

Example:

```text
USER
 │
 ├── HAS_SKILL ──→ CANVA
 │
 ├── WANTS_TO_LEARN ──→ EXCEL
 │
 ├── CREATED ──→ PROJECT
 │
 ├── LOCATED_IN ──→ MUMBAI
 │
 └── INTERESTED_IN ──→ BUSINESS
```

Relationships can then be used to identify relevant connections.

For example:

```text
Riya
  │
  ├── wants to learn Canva
  │
  ├── speaks Hindi
  │
  ├── lives in Mumbai
  │
  └── wants to start a business
          │
          ↓
      Priya
          │
          ├── Canva Expert
          ├── Digital Marketing
          ├── Hindi
          └── Mumbai
```

This creates a more meaningful recommendation than simple keyword matching.
